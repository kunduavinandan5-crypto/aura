Aura AI Study Assistant

Aura is an AI study assistant for school and college students. Ask questions by text or voice, snap a photo of a problem for step-by-step help, and keep your chats synced across devices.

Frequently asked questions

What is Aura?

Aura is an AI study assistant for school and college students. It explains concepts step by step, solves problems from photos, answers spoken questions, and keeps your chats synced across devices.

Can Aura solve a problem from a photo?

Yes. Upload or capture a photo of a math, science or diagram question and Aura walks through the solution step by step.

Does Aura support voice questions?

Yes. Tap the microphone to ask a question out loud in browsers that support speech recognition, and have answers read back to you.

Is my study data private?

Your chats are stored in your own account and protected with per-user access rules. You can delete all chat history and search records from Settings at any time.

Can I install Aura on my phone?

Yes. Aura is a progressive web app: use "Add to Home Screen" in your mobile browser to install it like a native app.
